Work

In 2026, China continues to dominate solar energy development, focusing on massive desert installations, technological innovation, and stabilizing a market suffering from severe overcapacity. While expected to add 180–240 GW of new capacity in 2026, growth is slowing due to intense price competition that triggered a 2025 industry crisis, leading to factory production cuts.

Key developments in China's solar expansion include:

  • Massive Manufacturing Dominance
  • Desert "Solar Walls"
  • Technological Advancement
  • Infrastructure Investment
  • Phasing Out Subsidies for Scale
